Favourites and labels: First enable Hotkey > Enable extended clipping actions and 500-item history in Preferences. Then open Jumpcut's alternate clipping menu (for example, right-click or Shift-click the menu bar icon, depending on your preferences), hover the clipping, and choose Add to Favourites, Make Bold, Make Italic, or a color from Color. Favourite clippings are marked with [F], and bold, italic, and color labels are visible in the main menu, alternate menu, and Favourites submenu.
